HOW IT WORKS

Inside the model

Text is normalized, converted into TF-IDF features and classified by a trained model.

1 · Preprocessing

Lowercase conversion, URL and number normalization, and punctuation cleanup.

2 · TF-IDF

Words and two-word phrases are converted into weighted numerical features.

3 · Classification

Naive Bayes and Logistic Regression are compared; the higher spam F1-score model is saved.

Training evaluation

{{ metrics }}
